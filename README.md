# Tic Tac Toe

A modern web-based Tic Tac Toe game with Player vs Player and Player vs AI modes. The AI is designed to provide a medium to hard challenge, making the game engaging for most users.

## Features
- **Player vs Player** and **Player vs AI** modes
- Responsive and visually appealing UI
- Medium-Hard AI: The AI is not trivial and offers a challenging experience
- New Game button for quick restarts
- Stylish design with custom favicon

## How to Play
1. Select your preferred mode (Player vs Player or Player vs AI).
2. The game board will appear. X always starts first.
3. Click on a box to make your move.
4. The game will announce the winner or a draw.
5. Click "New Game" to play again.

## Setup
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.

No additional dependencies are required.
